# Security Policy

If you believe you have found a security issue in Buda, do not open a public GitHub issue.

Please email the report to:

security@buda.ai

Include enough detail for the team to reproduce and assess the issue:

- Affected product area or URL
- Steps to reproduce
- Impact
- Screenshots or logs, if relevant
- Your preferred contact method

For product documentation, pricing, or account support questions, use hello@buda.ai instead.
