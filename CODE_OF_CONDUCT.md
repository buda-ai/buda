# Code of Conduct

We want Buda-related public spaces to be useful, direct, and respectful.

Please:

- Be specific and constructive.
- Keep discussions relevant to Buda, agents, skills, marketplace, docs, and public materials.
- Do not post private credentials, customer data, security details, or personal information.
- Do not harass, threaten, or abuse other participants.

Maintainers may close, hide, or remove content that does not fit these guidelines.
